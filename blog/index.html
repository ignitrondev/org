4 Years of LDR: The Story Behind IgnitronDev

IgnitronDev was not just built from code — it was built from distance, patience, and love. This is the story of Bima Adam Nugraha and Ririn Setiawati, co-founders and partners in life, who have been in a long-distance relationship since December 2021.

December 2021

Bima Adam Nugraha and Ririn Setiawati started their relationship. Two people from different worlds — a developer building platforms, and a girl from Narmada, Lombok Barat who would become his biggest inspiration.

2022 – 2023

Distance never stopped them. While Bima was deep in code — building fullstack systems, learning IoT, and dreaming of a platform factory — Ririn was the constant reminder of why it all mattered. Every late-night session had a purpose.

2024

IgnitronDev was born. Bima officially named Ririn as Co-Founder & CEO — not just as a title, but as recognition that behind every line of code, every platform shipped, and every partner onboarded, her presence and support made it possible.

2026 — Still Going Strong

4+ years of LDR. Still together. Still building. Ririn Setiawati, born November 28, 2007 in Narmada, Lombok Barat, is the Co-Founder & CEO of IgnitronDev. She is currently studying Nursing (Keperawatan) at Universitas Qamarul Huda Badaruddin Bagu (UNIQBHA), Lombok — proving that the best partnerships are built on trust, patience, and love, not just proximity.

"Distance is just a test to see how far love can travel. Ours has been traveling since December 2021 — and it's still going."

— Bima Adam Nugraha, Founder

Kenapa Bima Adam Suka Musik Atmospheric Phonk, Nightdrive, dan Space Ambient

Bagi Bima Adam, musik atmospheric bukan sekadar genre — ini adalah tempat pulang. Dari coding malam, LDR, overthinking, hingga refleksi diri. Inilah cerita di balik playlist-nya.

Bima Adam adalah seorang fullstack developer asal Boyolali yang dikenal menyukai dunia teknologi, Linux, coding malam, dan musik dengan nuansa atmospheric, phonk, ambient, nightdrive, hingga space aesthetic.

Bagi Bima, musik bukan hanya hiburan. Musik adalah bagian dari identitas, memori emosional, dan tempat untuk reconnect dengan dirinya sendiri.

Sejak lama, Bima sering mendengarkan musik instrumental atmospheric ketika sedang coding larut malam, fokus mengerjakan proyek, overthinking, menikmati suasana malam, refleksi hidup, hingga saat sedang merasa kehilangan arah.

Kenapa Bima Suka Musik Atmospheric Phonk?

Genre seperti atmospheric phonk, ambient drift, dan nightdrive music memberikan perasaan yang berbeda dibanding musik biasa. Musik ini memberikan rasa tenang, nostalgia, ruang untuk berpikir, fokus saat coding, dan sensasi seperti "kembali menjadi diri sendiri".

Saat hidup terasa kacau, musik-musik ini menjadi tempat aman untuk menenangkan pikiran.

Cocok didengarkan saat: coding malam, naik motor malam, hujan, perjalanan jauh, overthinking, atau saat ingin sendiri.

Bima Adam is a fullstack developer from Boyolali, known for his love of technology, Linux, late-night coding sessions, and music with atmospheric, phonk, ambient, nightdrive, and space aesthetic vibes.

For Bima, music is more than entertainment. It's part of his identity, emotional memory, and a way to reconnect with himself.

For a long time, Bima has been listening to atmospheric instrumental music while coding late at night, focusing on projects, overthinking, enjoying the night atmosphere, reflecting on life, or when feeling lost.

Why Does Bima Like Atmospheric Phonk?

Genres like atmospheric phonk, ambient drift, and nightdrive music feel different from regular music. They provide a sense of calm, nostalgia, space to think, focus while coding, and the sensation of "returning to yourself".

When life feels chaotic, these tracks become a safe place to quiet the mind.
