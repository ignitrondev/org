Engineering approach

Take the system
seriously.

A system is not complete at deployment. It should be understandable when it changes, observable when it fails, and practical for the people who operate it.

01

Start with constraints

Architecture follows the operational, technical, and human realities of the work.

02

Prefer explicitness

Interfaces, ownership, deployment paths, and trade-offs should be visible to the people who maintain them.

03

Leave behind capability

Good engineering makes future change safer and gives teams a clearer system to operate.
