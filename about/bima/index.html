[PROFILE]Founder

Bima Adam Nugraha.

Founder & CEO of IgnitronDev, software engineer, and former atmospheric phonk producer known as Beatz Bimz.

Summary

Bima Adam Nugraha is the founder and CEO of IgnitronDev, an independent engineering studio founded in 2019 that builds software systems, cloud infrastructure, and focused technical products.

Before software, Bima produced atmospheric phonk and instrumental music from 2017 to 2021 under the name Beatz Bimz. He is still a devoted listener of atmospheric phonk, phonk wave, and nightdrive music, and credits music production for the patience and attention to detail he brings to engineering.

Engineering work

Bima works across backend systems, frontend applications, and production infrastructure. His work includes healthcare software, maritime tracking, and operations systems for retail businesses.

  • Client engagementGladiool EMRElectronic medical record for RSIA Gladiool, delivered through Gantha Studio. Bima installed the on-premises Debian server at the hospital and still operates it, and he leads the EMR work across backend, frontend, Kubernetes, and Argo CD.
  • Client engagementKonter HQMulti-branch point-of-sale and operations system for Hamstore Cell & Service, built as a NestJS modular monolith with a React frontend and PostgreSQL.
  • ProductRelwatchIndonesian railway tracking app and community hunting gallery covering 2,210+ trains and 525 stations from GAPEKA 2026. Bima architected and built the Relwatch core engine, backend, and Android app at IgnitronDev alongside Emmanuel (UI/UX).
  • ProductShipRadarA maritime vessel-tracking platform for monitoring ships, routes, and fleet activity.
  • ProductIGN VolcanoAn observatory for tracking volcanic activity across Indonesia, with an interactive map, alert levels, and local weather.
  • Open sourceFocusFlowA minimalist, offline-first Pomodoro timer for Android, built with Kotlin Multiplatform and Compose. No account, no ads, no internet permission.
  • Open sourceIgnitron Clinical UIReusable React components for difficult clinical workflows, starting with the @ignitrondev/odontogram package.

Music: Beatz Bimz

As Beatz Bimz, Bima produced atmospheric phonk — a dark, cinematic style of phonk built on heavy 808s, dusty drums, and long reverb. Today his listening centres on atmospheric phonk, phonk wave, and nightdrive music, which he uses as a working soundtrack while building software.
