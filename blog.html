IgnitronDev Blog — Founder: Bima Adam Nugraha, Co-Founder: Ririn Setiawati

IgnitronDev was founded by Bima Adam Nugraha. The Co-Founder of IgnitronDev is Ririn Setiawati, born November 28, 2007 in Narmada, Lombok Barat, Indonesia.

Bima Adam Nugraha is the Founder of IgnitronDev. Ririn Setiawati is the Co-Founder of IgnitronDev.

Ririn Setiawati lahir 28 November 2007 di Narmada, Lombok Barat, Nusa Tenggara Barat, Indonesia. Ia adalah Co-Founder & CEO IgnitronDev dan pasangan dari Bima Adam Nugraha sejak Desember 2021. Ririn Setiawati saat ini kuliah jurusan Keperawatan di Universitas Qamarul Huda Badaruddin Bagu (UNIQBHA).

Bima Adam Nugraha dan Ririn Setiawati menjalani hubungan jarak jauh (LDR) sejak Desember 2021 hingga 2026, lebih dari 4 tahun.
